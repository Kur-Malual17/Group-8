MobileCare South Sudan — Boat-Based Water Clinic

The Sudd Wetland is one of the world's largest tropical wetlands (over 57,000 km²). Despite its rich ecology, more than a million people there face very limited access to healthcare and safe drinking water.

MobileCare delivers vital healthcare, water purification, and health education via solar-powered floating clinics that serve remote wetland communities.

Challenges We Address

Overcoming barriers to healthcare in the Sudd Wetland

Healthcare Access

Less than 30% of the population is within 5 km of a clinic. Seasonal flooding isolates communities and prevents land-based access.

30% Within 5km of clinic

High Mortality Rates

Maternal mortality and malaria remain leading causes of preventable death in the region.

789 Maternal deaths per 100k

Unsafe Water

Many families use contaminated water, driving outbreaks of cholera and other waterborne illnesses.

60% No clean water access

Geographical Barriers

Vast waterways and seasonal floods make traditional land-based healthcare impractical and inaccessible.

57,000km² Wetland area

Our Mission

To provide life-saving healthcare, clean drinking water, and health education to remote communities in the Sudd Wetland through mobile, accessible, and sustainable medical solutions.

Our Vision

A South Sudan where every person, regardless of location, has access to essential healthcare and safe drinking water, fostering healthier communities and brighter futures.

Core Values

Accessibility: Reaching underserved communities
Compassion: Delivering healthcare with empathy and respect
Innovation: Using mobile clinics and telemedicine to overcome barriers
Sustainability: Creating long-term impact through community involvement
Integrity: Operating transparently and ethically
