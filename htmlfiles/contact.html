Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about MobileCare

How can I support MobileCare's work?

You can support us through financial donations, in-kind contributions of medical supplies, volunteering your time and expertise, or by helping us spread awareness about our work. Visit our donation page or contact our partnerships team for more information.

Do you accept international volunteers?

Yes, we welcome international volunteers with relevant medical, technical, or operational expertise. All volunteers undergo a screening process and orientation. Contact our volunteer coordinator for current opportunities and requirements.

How are donations used?

Donations are primarily used for medical supplies, clinic maintenance and fuel, staff training, and community health programs. We maintain full financial transparency and provide regular reports to our donors about how their contributions are making an impact.

Can organizations partner with MobileCare?

Absolutely! We actively seek partnerships with organizations that share our mission. Partnerships can take various forms including funding, technical expertise, resource sharing, or collaborative programs. Contact our CEO or partnerships team to discuss potential collaboration.
