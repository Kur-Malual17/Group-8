Our Journey

Key milestones in our mission to transform healthcare

2022

Program Launch

MobileCare South Sudan launched with our first floating clinic, serving 3 remote communities in the Sudd Wetland.

2,500+ patients served 3 communities reached
2023

Expansion & Partnerships

Added two more clinics and established partnerships with WHO and UNICEF, expanding our reach to 12 communities.

7,800+ patients served 5 new partnerships
2024

Technology Integration

Implemented telemedicine services and digital health records, improving care coordination and patient outcomes.

9,600+ patients served Telemedicine launched
2025

Sustainable Growth

Expanded to 28 communities with 6 active clinics, focusing on sustainable operations and community empowerment.

12,450+ patients served 28 communities reached
